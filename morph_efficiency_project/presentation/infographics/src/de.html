Umlaut accompanies noun plurals, Konjunktiv II, comparatives, and some derivations. The engine scans right-to-left for the last umlautable vowel, tries un-umlauting, and checks against the ~8,000 stem dictionary.

Live Decomposition

Watch the engine analyze "Krankenhauses" — compound splitting is the distinctive German step.

Input

Krankenhauses "of the hospital"
Step A Inflectional Stripping
Krankenhaus + -es → GEN.NEUT

Strip genitive suffix -es. Tags: case=GEN, gender=NEUT, num=SG, POS = NOUN

Step B Compound Splitting
Kranken + Haus
Kranken (sick, modifier) Haus (house, HEAD)

Compound split via Fugenelement detection. Kranken = krank + -en (linking element). Right-headed: Haus determines gender (NEUT) and POS (NOUN).

Step C Root Extraction
Haus ROOT (head)
prefix = Kranken case=GEN gender=NEUT num=SG

Final root: Haus. Chain: COMPOUND(Kranken+Haus). The compound splitting step is what makes German analysis distinctively complex.

Tokenization Impact

Why This Matters for AI

German compounds are where morph-aware tokenization shines. BPE fragments them arbitrarily; morphology splits them at meaningful boundaries.

BPE Tokenization

"Das Krankenhaus ist geschlossen"

Das _Krank en haus _ist _gesch lossen

7 tokens

Morph-Aware Tokenization

"Das Krankenhaus ist geschlossen"

Das [DET.NEUT] Kranken+Haus [NOUN] ist [AUX.3SG] geschlossen [PERF]

4 tokens + tags

~12%

Expected efficiency gain from morph-aware tokenization

Compound splitting is the biggest win. BPE fractures compounds at arbitrary byte-pair boundaries; morph-aware splitting preserves semantic component boundaries.
