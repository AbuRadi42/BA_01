Single Blocks on a Shelf

How Mandarin Builds Words

Mandarin words carry almost no internal structure. Each character sits as an independent block — no assembly, no attachments.

Sentence

孩子们在看书 — "The children are reading books"

孩子 háizi NOUN
们 men PL
在 zài ADP
看 kàn VERB
书 shū NOUN

Each word sits independently. No conjugation, no declension, no agreement. Contrast this with fusional languages where parts fuse together inside a single word.

In Arabic, a single verb encodes 6–8 features. In Mandarin, the verb 看 encodes zero. Tense, aspect, and mood live in separate particles, not inside the word.

The Mandarin pipeline is the shortest of all nine engines. That brevity is the entire point.

Step-by-Step

Live Decomposition

Watch the engine analyze 孩子们 (children). Notice how minimal this is.

Input

孩子们 háizimen — "children"
Step A Inflectional Stripping
孩子 + 们 → PL

Strip 们 (plural marker, animate nouns only). Tags: num=PL, POS = NOUN

Step B Derivational Detection
孩 + 子 → NOMINALIZER

Detect 子 (nominalizer suffix, lexicalized). Chain: 子→NOMINALIZER. Root = 孩

Result Root Extraction
孩 ROOT

That’s it. Two steps. In Arabic, a comparable analysis would take 5+ steps with root extraction, pattern matching, and feature fusion.

Tag Inventory

What the Engine Can Tag

~20 unique feature bundles

The grid below is deliberately sparse. Most categories that exist in other languages simply do not exist in Mandarin. That is the point.

Part of Speech

NOUN VERB ADJ ADV PRON DET ADP CONJ PART NUM CLF AUX UNKNOWN

Aspect Particles

aspect=PERF (了) aspect=DUR (着) aspect=EXP (过) aspect=PROG

This is the entirety of Mandarin verbal "inflection." 4 aspect values via free particles.

Pronoun / Number

num=PL person=1/2/3 gender=MASC/FEM/NEUT

Gender is a written distinction only (他/她/它). Not grammatical gender — no agreement.

Sentence-Final Particles

QUESTION (吗) TOPIC (呢) SUGGESTION (吧) EMPHASIS

Structural Roles

role=ATTR (的) role=ADVL (地) role=COMP (得) construction=BA construction=BEI

Tags That Do NOT Exist

tense mood voice case definiteness agreement

These categories exist in other engines but have no Mandarin equivalent. This absence is the most important diagnostic.

Tokenization Impact

Why This Matters for AI

Mandarin is the experimental control. BPE and morph-aware tokenization produce nearly identical results — because there is nothing to decompose.

BPE Tokenization

孩子们在看书

孩子 们 在 看 书

5 tokens

Morph-Aware Tokenization

孩子们在看书

孩子 +PL 在 看 书

4 tokens + tags

~0%

Expected efficiency gain from morph-aware tokenization

This is the most important data point in the experiment. Near-zero improvement confirms that gains in other languages come from morphological compression specifically.
