Suffix Ordering Problem

Why does the engine try -aron before -o? Because greedy shortest-first matching would misparse every preterite plural. The engine uses longest suffix first.

Clitic Unstacking

The engine peels clitics from right to left, reconstructing the verb form underneath. Each layer reveals a pronoun clitic and its grammatical role.

Why This Matters for AI

BPE tokenizers split Spanish words at arbitrary byte boundaries, destroying morphological structure. Morph-aware tokenization preserves it.

BPE Tokenization

dijéramos
dij ér amos

3 tokens. No grammatical boundaries preserved. The irregular stem dij- is split from its morphological context.

Morph-Aware Tokenization

dijéramos
dij- -éramos

2 tokens. Clean split at the morpheme boundary. The model sees stem + SUBJ.IMPERF.1PL.

~20-30%

Predicted alignment improvement for Spanish

Spanish’s fusional morphology means each suffix carries multiple features. BPE often splits these fused endings incorrectly. Morph-aware tokenization is expected to deliver moderate but meaningful gains — less dramatic than Turkish (56.9%) because Spanish has fewer suffix slots, but significantly more than English where BPE already performs well.
