Extension Stacking

Verb extensions stack onto the root in a fixed order. The engine strips from the outermost extension inward, recognizing each one.

pik-a cook (bare root)
pik-w-a be cooked PASS
pik-ish-a make cook CAUS
pik-ish-w-a be made to cook CAUS+PASS
pik-i-w-a be cooked for APPL+PASS

The engine strips -w- first (outermost), then -ish- or -i-, arriving at the root pik.
